What is sourced, and what is owed

A blank field is a finding. A fabricated one is a defect.

No date in this reference was carried over from a programme’s usual cycle or filled in from memory. A record carries a deadline only when something was actually read to obtain it, and the URL that was read is recorded in sourced_from. Everything else is blank and marked date not sourced, which the module groups into its own band rather than hiding.

Records marked sourced were read off a named third party, not the organisation’s own page — the container this was built in cannot reach most of those pages directly. Primary confirmation is still owed on every one of them. Raise a record to confirmed by reading the guidelines yourself and setting date_basis, verified and sourced_from in data/open-calls/. The build refuses any record that claims more than it can show.

Stage moves and drafts made here live for the life of the tab. Nothing is stored in the browser and nothing is transmitted. No applicant-identifying data belongs in this repository, in the registry, or in anything exported from it.
