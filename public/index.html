Four screens. One venue. No desktop site in a smaller box. Four screens. One venue.

ayeyoty.co is built strictly for Apple hardware — iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad and MacBook. Each one gets its own floor: the layout changes, the input model changes, and so does how much of the venue you are shown. The page you are reading has already decided which floor you are standing on.

Take it with you

Installed, the venue opens in its own window with no browser chrome, keeps the safe area it was designed around, and gets its own icon.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch it from the Home Screen — it opens standalone, status bar translucent.

MacBook

  1. Open this page in Safari 17 or later.
  2. Choose File › Add to Dock.
  3. It launches as its own app, with its own window and its own Dock icon.

Apple Watch

  1. There is nothing to install. The watch opens web content from a link in Mail or Messages and hands it a very narrow viewport.
  2. The doorway floor exists for exactly that arrival: one screen, one action, no chrome.
