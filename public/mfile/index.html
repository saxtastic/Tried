It does not run between the officers. It runs across all of them, on one input. M file

Four questions asked of every file — what is iterative, what is duplicate, what is novel, what is functional. Three of them a scan can answer. One of them no scan can answer, at any resolution, ever, and saying so is the finding rather than the gap.

The four What it needs from you

The four questions

Duplicate

Two records are duplicates when their content hashes are equal. Same name is not duplication. Same size is not duplication. Without a hash it will say candidate and refuse the word.

Iterative

Same normalised stem, different content, ordered by time — the newest is the head and the rest are its history. Normalisation is the whole question, and it is read off your own filenames rather than guessed from a list of suffixes.

Novel

Unique content and a stem that appears once. Unique content is a fact about bytes; novel is a claim about the work, and a work can be unique in content while being the fourth attempt at the same thing.

Functional

Is anything referring to it — a page, a kit, a release, a send? This is the one the filesystem cannot answer, because bytes, names and dates are all internal to the file and function is a relation to something else. It reports unknown, and it never reports unused.

What it needs before it will speak

One folder

Point at the folder that is already the worst of it. One real folder beats a taxonomy; everything after that is measured instead of imagined.

Three filenames

Three names of yours that are the same work at different stages. That is the ruleset. Three examples of your cadence are worth more than any convention, because the cadence is the thing you said was unreliable.

One definition

When a file is still doing something — what is it doing? Named in a kit, on a page, in a release, sent to someone? That sentence is the only thing that can answer the fourth question.

What it will not do

Move anything

It never renames, moves, archives or deletes, under any answer. It reads and it reports. Both halves are asserted by a test that greps the source for a write.

Reach a store it was not pointed at

No network request, no dependency. The scanner runs where the files already are — a Mac with iCloud Drive mounted, a disk, a synced folder — and hands back one JSON file. An iCloud placeholder is recorded as not-downloaded rather than pulled.

Estimate a verdict

Every answer carries a basis. A verdict that cannot reach confirmed or derived is returned at the degraded wording, never at the confident one — because a guessed verdict on a file store is worse than none. It gets acted on.
