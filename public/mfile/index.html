It does not run between the officers. It runs across all of them, on one input. M file

Four questions asked of every file — what is iterative, what is duplicate, what is novel, what is functional. Three of them a scan can answer. One of them no scan can answer, at any resolution, ever, and saying so is the finding rather than the gap. Audio, video, images and documents all count, through two doors — a folder you grant, and the Photos library, which affords capture date, duration, dimensions and album membership that a folder scan cannot see. The figures below are a worked example on invented records, not a scan of anything real.

    What it needs before it will speak

    One folder

    Point at the folder that is already the worst of it. One real folder beats a taxonomy; everything after that is measured instead of imagined.

    Three filenames

    Three names of yours that are the same work at different stages. That is the ruleset. Three examples of your cadence are worth more than any convention, because the cadence is the thing you said was unreliable.

    One definition

    When a file is still doing something — what is it doing? Named in a kit, on a page, in a release, sent to someone? That sentence is the only thing that can answer the fourth question.
