What these records are, and are not

Every open call in this reference is a real programme, and every Guidelines link goes to the organisation that runs it. No date or award figure here has been confirmed against its source. They are placeholders derived from each programme’s usual cycle, marked estimated wherever they appear, and they must not be relied on. Open the guidelines and read the real deadline before you plan against it.

Confirm a record by editing its file under data/open-calls/: set the true closes date, change date_basis to confirmed, put today in verified, and raise register from V to D. The build refuses any record that claims verification it does not have.
