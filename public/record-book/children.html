The child

Harm to children is not a category of entry in this book. It is a stage in most of them. 11 of 25 entries record an injury that reached a child as a child, in 5 of the 6 periods the log covers.

Every other field here assumes an adult with capacity — a claimant who can assert, a forum that can hear. harmed.children exists because that assumption fails at exactly the point where most of these injuries begin, and because the field it requires — what the child carried into adulthood — is where the harm is actually collected.

The two systems

Two institutions reach a child before any court does, and this book now carries 4 entries in which one of them is the actor rather than the setting.

  • Schooling. The anti-literacy statutes made teaching a child a crime, and were enacted directly after revolts on the express reasoning that literacy made a person ungovernable. Prince Edward County closed every public school for five years rather than admit Black children to them. Clyde Kennard was imprisoned for applying to a college. In each the actor is a legislature, a county board, or a state agency.
  • Medicine. The Public Health Service ran a forty-year study on men it deceived. Eugenics boards and federally funded programmes sterilised women and girls, two of them aged fourteen and twelve, on a consent their mother could not read.

The two meet. The classification that selected many of those sterilised — feeblemindedness — was manufactured by intelligence testing applied to Black children in schools, and the operation was then performed by a health system acting on that classification. The child passed from one institution to the other, and the record of the first became the warrant for the second.

A person kept from literacy as a child could not read a bill of sale, a labour contract, a ballot, or a summons as an adult. The statutes therefore reached forward into every later transaction that required reading, which is most of the ones this book records - including the sharecropping contracts of the Elaine entry and the mother's mark on the consent form in the sterilisation entry.

A girl sterilised at twelve or fourteen carries the whole of the injury into an adulthood she did not consent to enter that way. The classification that selected many of them - feebleminded - was itself produced by the intelligence-testing apparatus applied to Black children in schools, so the school and the operating table were connected by a single pedagogy.

Prince Edward County closes every public school for five years civil rights

denied schoolingseparated from family to obtain schoolingaged out of schooling

Children who lost five years of schooling did not get them back. Many never completed secondary education, and the county's Black adults carried measurably lower attainment and earnings for the rest of their working lives. This is the clearest single demonstration in the corpus of the point that the injury begins in childhood and is collected in adulthood.
