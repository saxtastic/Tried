Language

Words whose definition did operational work — what each one meant, what deploying it accomplished, and how that is confirmed from consequence rather than from the text.

Three things are kept apart here, and collapsing them is how a record gets falsified. What a word denotes is dictionary work. What a speaker did by choosing it lives in the contrast set — the word that was available and not used. And the deployment is never confirmed from the word itself: it is confirmed from what the word licensed. Greenwood was a riot in every official record until 2001, and fire policies of the period carried riot-exclusion clauses. The word was not a description of the event. It was the instrument that voided the claims.

10 terms carry 28 distinct senses here (2.8 each). A term earns an entry only if choosing it over an available alternative changed what could be claimed, paid, barred or recorded, and the validator rejects any term with fewer than two senses, any reading with nothing that could defeat it, and any container term that does not state its own cost. 9 of 10 are confirmed by consequence rather than by reading.

Container terms

An aggregating word buys a count and charges for it. Black aggregates Persons subjected to a classification, across instruments that did not define it the same way. It buys: A harm becomes countable across eras, so a continuity can be shown rather than a series of unrelated incidents. It hides: Which class each instrument actually named, and the fact that the classification was imposed by the same authorities the record accuses. Cost: Precision at the level of the individual claimant. A count taken from the 1890 schedule and a count taken from the 2020 schedule are not counts of the same defined class, and any series that crosses that boundary must say so.

riot

When an armed body destroys a district and the record calls it a riot, what does that word do that 'massacre' would not?

Fire policies of the period carried riot-exclusion clauses. Classifying Greenwood a riot voided the insurance claims of the people whose property burned; the sole recovery on record went to a white claimant for guns taken. What would show this reading is wrong
Contemporaneous policies without riot-exclusion clauses, or claims paid to Black policyholders under the riot classification, would show the word was descriptive rather than operative.

compensation

Compensation to whom? The word names a payment without naming its recipient, and in the one instance the United States enacted it for slavery, the recipient was not the enslaved.

SenseGlossDeployed byWhat it does
indemnity_to_titleholderPayment to an owner for property lost by operation of law. Congress in 1862; Parliament in 1837Treats emancipation as a taking from the holder, and settles the account with him.
indemnity_to_the_harmedPayment to a person for an injury done to them. Claimants, later redress statutesTreats the enslaved person as the injured party rather than the subject matter.
Contested axis
Who the injured party is. Both senses are ordinary legal English; they differ only in who is standing in the position of the wronged.
Confirmed by
operational consequence — What the choice licensed, paid, barred or voided. The strongest method, because it is a fact about the world rather than about the text.
contrast set — Which available alternative was not used.
Evidence
The District of Columbia Compensated Emancipation Act of 16 April 1862 appropriated funds to slaveholders on petition, adjudicated by a board of commissioners, and separately offered a sum to any freed person who agreed to emigrate. Payment flowed to the holder; the freed person was offered money to leave. The contrast set is exact: Congress had the word, the mechanism and the appropriation, and directed all three at title.
What would show this reading is wrong
An 1862-64 federal appropriation paid directly to formerly enslaved people as indemnity for their enslavement would defeat this reading. None is on file.

Black

The corpus counts a harm across two hundred and thirty years using one word, while the instruments that recorded the harm changed what the word picked out almost every decade.

SenseGlossDeployed byWhat it does
property_classIn slave schedules, a countable holding recorded by age, sex and colour under an owner's name - frequently without a personal name at all. Census enumerators 1850 and 1860; bills of sale; coastwise manifestsEstablishes the transaction while withholding the person, which is why the ledger proves the trafficking and cannot supply the identity.
graded_admixtureBlack, mulatto, quadroon, octoroon - the 1890 schedule's four-way division by fraction of ancestry. The 1890 censusMakes the category a measurement of descent, administered by an enumerator's judgement rather than a subject's statement.
hypodescentAny African ancestry, by statute. Virginia's Racial Integrity Act of 1924 is the type case. State legislatures and registrarsConverts a spectrum into a binary for the purpose of marriage, school and franchise law, and makes the boundary a criminal matter.
self_identificationA category the person selects; from 2000, selectable in combination. The census from 1960 onwardReturns the classification to the classified, and thereby breaks comparability with every prior series.
Contested axis
Who holds the pen. Every sense change is a change in who was authorised to assign the category, and the category was assigned long before it was claimed.
Confirmed by
contemporaneous usage — How the word was used by others at that moment, not now.
later revision — An institution changed the word later, which discloses what the old one was doing.
operational consequence — What the choice licensed, paid, barred or voided. The strongest method, because it is a fact about the world rather than about the text.
Evidence
The federal schedules are dated and public. The categories are not stable across them, and the instruments state who did the assigning: enumerator judgement through 1950, self-report from 1960, combination from 2000.
What would show this reading is wrong
A single federal instrument applying one stable definition of the class from 1790 to the present would defeat the claim that the container is administratively constructed.

sovereign

The word claims a kind of authority. Which kind, and against whom, decides whether an instrument is evidence or is void on its face.

SenseGlossDeployed byWhat it does
state_attributeThe supreme authority of a polity, from which jurisdiction and immunity both descend. Courts, legislaturesSupplies the ladder this book calls echelon, and supplies sovereign immunity, which is an impediment in the corpus.
personhoodThe condition of belonging to oneself - the interest that chattel enslavement destroys at its root. Narratives, abolitionist argument, the human-rights instruments in rights.jsonNames the injury at the level where it happened, before any question of forum.
pseudo_juridical_claimAn asserted individual exemption from the jurisdiction of courts and statutes, advanced through filings styled as instruments. Contested axis
Whether the word describes a relation the speaker has to themselves, or a relation they assert against a court. The first is the injury; the third forecloses the remedy.
Confirmed by
operational consequence — What the choice licensed, paid, barred or voided. The strongest method, because it is a fact about the world rather than about the text.

The third sense is confirmed by what it produces on a docket: dismissal on the pleadings and, in a run of cases, sanction. incident

The flattening word. It gives an event a boundary in time and denies it a place in a series.

SenseGlossDeployed byWhat it does
bounded_occurrenceA discrete happening, complete in itself. Official summaries, press shorthandSevers the event from anything before or after it, so no pattern is available to be found and no continuity is on the record.
instance_of_a_practiceOne occurrence of a repeated and describable practice. Statistical series, commissions, this book's era fieldMakes the practice the object, so a count becomes possible and a continuity can be shown.
Contested axis
Whether the event is singular or serial. Nothing about the event itself decides this; the choice is made in the writing.
Confirmed by
contrast set — Which available alternative was not used.
later revision — An institution changed the word later, which discloses what the old one was doing.
Evidence
Where a source describes an occurrence without reference to any comparable occurrence it knew of, the first sense is operating. Periodisation is this book's answer: era is carried on every entry precisely so that no entry can be read as an incident.
What would show this reading is wrong
A source using the singular sense while itself compiling the series would show the word is not doing the severing.

reparation

The word is treated as one demand. It is at least three, and they have different addressees, different instruments and different costs.

SenseGlossDeployed byWhat it does
restitutionThe thing taken is returned, or its value is. Property claims, land returnRequires an identified holding and an identified successor, which is what the destruction of records was for.
compensationA sum is paid for an injury that cannot be undone. Redress statutes, claim billsRequires only a definable class and an appropriation - the form every enacted programme in precedents.json has taken.
atonementThe wrong is acknowledged and the relation altered. Apologies, resolutions, commissionsCosts nothing and returns nothing, and is the form most frequently supplied in place of the other two.
Contested axis
Which of the three is being refused when reparations are called impossible. The corpus indicates the objection attaches to the second and is argued as though it attached to the first.
Confirmed by
operational consequence — What the choice licensed, paid, barred or voided. The strongest method, because it is a fact about the world rather than about the text.
contrast set — Which available alternative was not used.
Evidence
restoration.forms already separates these: full_restitution, partial_compensation, and symbolic. Across the corpus the third is the commonest and the first is never reached.
