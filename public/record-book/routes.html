Route of authority

Every actor stood somewhere on a ladder of authority — the holder and the overseer, the mob acting as their proxy, the town, the county, the state, and above them the federal government and the Supreme Court. Every matter either climbed that ladder or stopped. This page records where each one stopped and what stopped it, which is the nearest thing this book has to an appeal route.

The inverted track

4 entries carry a second route: the one the state ran against the people it had already injured. 3 of 4 climbed higher than the same entry's track against the perpetrators, and 3 reached the Supreme Court. Only one perpetrator track ever reached that rung, and it went there to be undone: Colfax, where the corpus's only convictions were vacated. The exception is Framing and imprisonment of Clyde Kennard, where the actor was itself an organ of the state and neither track left that level.
