How it decides

Two rules, both set by the operator, both visible here rather than buried.

Ranked, never filtered

Every card renders, including the ones ranked last, each carrying the reason it sits where it does. Two of the five rank low because nothing establishes them — not because the domain matters less. A threshold that hides a card is a decision made where nobody can see it, and a wrong threshold is invisible by construction.

The outcome belongs to the card

One card ends in a draft at a named path. One ends in captured answers and no document. One would end in something addressed and dated but queued, because sending stays a separate deliberate act. The system does not have an output type; each card does.

Blank rather than plausible

Finance and production render with their fields listed as blank and nothing invented to fill them. They have no question set either, because writing five plausible questions would be inventing the domain. The only honest question there is what the card should hold.

Nothing is stored

Answers live in memory for the life of the tab and leave as text you copy. No cookie, no local storage, no third-party request. An interview that survives the tab would be a record, and a record needs somewhere it was decided to keep it.
